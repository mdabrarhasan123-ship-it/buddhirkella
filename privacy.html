Privacy
policy.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What this publication may collect

PATHANTOR may process information you choose to submit, such as an email address when you explicitly subscribe to the newsletter, a display name and comment when you submit a comment, and account information handled by the platform's authentication service when you sign in.

Newsletter consent

Newsletter subscription is optional and separate from account sign-in. The site records the email address, subscription status, consent time and source so the preference can be managed. Newsletter delivery is not active until the email workflow is configured.

Comments and moderation

Comments are stored for moderation and are not displayed publicly until approved. Do not submit sensitive personal information in a public comment.

Technical data

The hosting platform may process operational logs needed to provide, protect and troubleshoot the site. Article-view analytics and deduplication may be limited or unavailable; this policy does not claim tracking that has not been implemented. The site does not intentionally display invented analytics.

Third-party services

Hosting, authentication and font services may process requests under their own privacy terms. Google sign-in is subject to owner-side OAuth configuration and may not be available on every domain.

Your choices

You may choose not to create an account or subscribe. For requests concerning subscriber information, comments, corrections or privacy, contact the editorial desk ↗.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the publication's features change. The publication should be reviewed before launch to ensure this text matches the final data flows and applicable law.
