Reuse &
licensing.

Each published article displays its own reuse licence. Do not assume every article or image is free to republish. If an article has no clear licence, contact the editorial desk before reproducing it.

Common licence options

CC0 1.0: A public-domain dedication where legally effective.

CC BY 4.0: Reuse and adaptation are allowed under the licence terms, with appropriate attribution and indication of changes.

CC BY-SA 4.0: Attribution is required, and adaptations must be shared under the same licence.

CC BY-NC 4.0: Attribution is required and use is limited to non-commercial purposes under the licence terms.

Custom permission: Follow the specific permission statement displayed with the article.

All rights reserved: Permission is required unless an exception or other legal right applies.

Third-party material

Images, excerpts, photographs, illustrations and embedded materials may be owned by third parties and may have different rights from the article text. Always check the article-specific licence and the source's terms.

Attribution and requests

When attribution is required, include the author if stated, article title, PATHANTOR as the publication, a link to the original, the licence name and a note about changes. For permissions or corrections, contact the editorial desk ↗.
